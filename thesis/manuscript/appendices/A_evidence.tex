\section{Access to the underlying project evidence}
The project references in the bibliography identify the dissertation's own experiment records. This appendix reproduces the principal numerical comparisons from the development narrative and explains their calculation. The accompanying \texttt{Thesis\_Review\_Evidence.zip} supplies byte-identical copies of selected reports, structured result files, configuration records and implementation modules. Its \texttt{manifest.json} records each file's original relative path, byte count and SHA-256 digest.

The package accompanies the PDF as an examination supplement. It has no DOI and is not yet a public repository deposit. Recipients can inspect the text and JSON records without access to the author's workstation. The verification script checks file integrity and recomputes the original history-confirmation means, supplementary single-classifier results and historical latency comparison from saved confusion counts. It does not retrain the detector or reproduce predictions from raw telemetry. The large datasets and serialized fitted models remain in the research archive, so auditing the supplied numerical records is distinct from an independent rerun.

\section{History confirmation: the complete original pairing}
The baseline and received-history system were evaluated on the same six sources for model seeds 701--705 after design selection. Table~\ref{tab:historypairs} reproduces all thirty replay pairs. For each cell, the review package supplies the original true-positive, false-positive and false-negative counts for replay and the other families, together with the pooled and false-alarm measures. The filename pattern is \texttt{protected\_history\_system\_v1/seed/SEED/confirmation.json}.

A cell's F1 is calculated as $2TP/(2TP+FP+FN)$. The reported comparison averages the thirty cell F1 values with equal weight. It is therefore different from concatenating the confusion counts before calculating F1. The original full-precision means are 0.706383226 and 0.730307195, respectively; the rounded main-text values are 0.7064 and 0.7303. All thirty paired differences are positive.

The selected history refinement is confirmed on this assessment population. The earlier 0.6757 router result belongs to a different confirmation population. The later extension, recorded in the final ten-seed release, adds five fits on the same six history-confirmation sources.

\section{Development decisions and their evidence scope}
The initial residual-history pilot added 56 columns and reduced replay F1 from 0.7173 to 0.6954 on the held-source TRAIN diagnostic. The subsequent received-history pilot improved it to 0.7432 on that diagnostic. Its remaining-fold replication showed replay gains in all twenty primary paired comparisons but failed the declared noise-protection gate. That failure remains part of the record. The protected full-system experiment was a subsequent design with its own calibration protections and confirmation, as represented in Table~\ref{tab:historypairs}.

The review package includes the pilot reports, the replication protocol and outcome, and the protected-system protocol and frozen selections. Blind-reference and temporal-specialist reports specify their own development populations. Earlier EVAL-3 feedback reports are separate from the later repeated-fit campaign. Readers can identify design decisions, failed gates and frozen evaluations from the records themselves, without having to infer their status from filenames.

\section{Checking the supplement}
Unzip the review package and run \texttt{python3 verify.py} from its root. The script checks every listed file against its manifest digest. It reconstructs the original thirty-pair replay means, verifies all 480 supplementary source/model/operating-point reports and their 80 frozen thresholds, and checks the historical zero- and three-hour counts. A successful hash check establishes consistency with the supplied package, not correctness of the underlying experiment by itself. A reviewer can then inspect the protocols, scoring code, other-family counts and configuration records directly.

The supplement is intended for examination of this manuscript. A permanent public release, including an archival identifier and decisions about larger data/model artifacts, remains a submission-stage task. Neither public deposit nor advisor approval is represented as completed.
