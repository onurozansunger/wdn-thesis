This appendix gives the complete tables for Section~\ref{sec:operating_sensitivity-supp20261008}. F1 values and false-positive percentages are rounded to four decimal places. Each network mean averages the same ten fitted models evaluated on six shared source datasets. F1, family macro F1 and worst-family F1 are calculated within each cell before averaging. The worst-family column is therefore the mean of cellwise minima. Shared sources limit the independence of these repeated evaluations, as discussed in Chapter~\ref{ch:discussion}.

\section{Comparison under common calibration constraints}

Each cap and objective is applied to both systems using calibration data. Hybrid, LGB3h and LGB0h denote the frozen hybrid, the primary three-hour LightGBM control and the contextual zero-hour LightGBM control. Evaluation FPR is measured after freezing the selected rules and can differ between systems or exceed the calibration cap. The balanced objective maximizes worst-family F1, not family macro F1.

\section{Frozen-rule component diagnostics}

All eight variants use the original thresholds. Branch removals retain the original verifier and external replay veto on remaining specialist alarms. Verifier bypass retains the external replay veto; replay-veto removal retains the verifier, including its original candidate-region behavior. Removing both guards admits raw branch alarms. General's internal mixture routing is unchanged wherever General alarms contribute. All model scores were computed for these diagnostics, but the specialists-only alarm rule does not consume General's output. Removing a specialist's final alarm contribution does not remove its score channels from the shared router, feedback and verifier inputs. These diagnostics describe the contribution of alarm rules to the fitted system; they do not isolate target-group exclusion or reference fitting. The separate factorial study in Section~\ref{sec:reference_factorial} varies exclusion and IRLS with downstream refitting, while keeping whitening and median aggregation fixed.
